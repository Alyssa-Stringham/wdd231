Frequently Asked Questions

How long do horses live?

On average, horses live between 25 and 30 years.

How much do horses cost?

The purchase price of a horse can be anywhere from free to over a million dollars. Most horses are between $5,000 and $15,000.

What is the difference between a horse and a pony?

With the exception of a few breeds, horses are over 14.2hh (4 ft 10 in) and ponies are 14.1hh (4 ft 9 in) or smaller

When were horses domesticated?

It is unknown exactly when horses were first domesticated and is therefore a subject of debate. However, it is generally agreed that horses were domesticated sometime between 3000BCE and 2200BCE.
