Interesting Facts and Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do horses live?

On average, horses live between 25 and 30 years. The oldest horse on record was Old Billy, who lived for 62 years (1760-1822).

How much do horses cost?

The purchase price of a horse can be anywhere from free to over a million dollars. Most horses are between $5,000 and $15,000. The most expensive horse ever sold was a Thoroughbred named Fusaichi Pegasus who sold for $70 million.

What is the difference between a horse and a pony?

With the exception of a few breeds, horses are 14.2hh (4 ft 10 in) or taller and ponies are 14.1hh (4 ft 9 in) or smaller.

When were horses domesticated?

It is unknown exactly when horses were first domesticated and is therefore a subject of debate. However, it is generally agreed that horses were domesticated sometime between 3000 BCE and 2200 BCE. The earliest recorded horse race took place in Greece during the 33rd Olympic Games of 648 BCE.

How high can horses jump?

According to the FEI, the record for the highest cleared jump is 8 feet 1.25 inches. The longest recorded jump was 27 feet 6.75 inches.

How fast are horses?

The average horse has a top speed between 25 and 30 mph. The fastest horse ever recorded was a Quarter Horse named A Long Goodbye who reached a top speed of 55mph.

How many bones do horses have?

Most horses have 205 bones in their body; Arabian's only have 201 bones.

What is the most popular horse breed in the United States?

The Quarter Horse is the most popular horse breed in the United States with an estimated population of 2.1 million horses - about 42% of the total US horse population

How many horses live in the United States?

According to the 2023 Economic Impact Study of the American Horse Council, there are about 6.65 million horses in the United States.
